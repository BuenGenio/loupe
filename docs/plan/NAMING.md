# App name: options

The name is not final. Quick web checks (2026-10-04) found **no email apps** with these names.
These are not trademark searches: do the checks at the bottom before deciding.

## Constraints

- **No "Thunderbird"** in the name, icon or domain, and no bird-plus-lightning imagery that could look like Mozilla's logo.
  True statements in words are allowed, such as "works with Thunderbird tags and filters" ([research](research/mobile-client-2026-10.md#2-trademark)).
- Short and easy to say, with no fixed meaning in mail or tech.
- Works as an iOS/Android app name and as a domain or handle, with a suffix if needed: `getkestrel.app`, `kestrelmail.org`.
- Fits the product: calm on the surface, sharp underneath; search and reading as the stars.

## Shortlist

| Name | Why it fits | Known conflicts | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Kestrel** | A small falcon that hovers and spots things from far away: *search*. A bird (a nod to the Thunderbird roots) but not a thunderbird. Two syllables; easy in most languages. | ASP.NET Core's web server is called Kestrel (a different field, but well known among developers). A web design mockup uses the name. | **Recommended.** |
| **Loupe** | A jeweller's magnifier: *reading* and *search*. Matches the magnifier in the Expression Search Reloaded logo, so the two products would share a brand. | GNOME's image viewer is called Loupe. So are Movable Type's analytics tool and a streaming app. Some people won't know how to say it. | **Strong alternative.** |
| **Heron** | Patient, precise, calm: the Apple Mail side. | Common in company names; check class 9 trademarks. | Good |
| **Osprey** | A hunter with excellent eyesight. | **Osprey Approach** sells an Outlook add-in for legal case management, too close for mail. Osprey is also a backpack brand. | Avoid for mail |
| **Dowse** | "To search for hidden things" (as in dowsing). Distinctive and short. | Nothing major found. Less familiar word; it sounds like "douse". | Fine |
| **Kittiwake** | A sea bird; playful and memorable. | Nothing major found. Three syllables, harder to spell. | Fine |

## Names to avoid

- **Anything with "Thunderbird"**, or "T-bird": trademark.
- **Tern**: *Sterna Mail* (`app.sterna`, a JMAP and IMAP mail client) already exists, and *Sterna* is Latin for tern.
- **Petrel**: too close to Huawei's *Petal Mail*.
- Existing mail apps: **Sparrow**, **Spark**, **Canary**, **Airmail**, **Postbox**, **Bluebird**, **Merlin**.

## Checks before deciding

1. Trademark registers, classes 9 (software) and 38/42 (communications and software services):
   [USPTO](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/), [EUIPO eSearch](https://euipo.europa.eu/eSearch/), [WIPO Global Brand Database](https://branddb.wipo.int/).
2. App Store and Google Play: search the exact name plus "mail".
3. Domain and handles: `.app`, `.org`, `.com` (with a suffix if needed), GitHub org, Mastodon or Bluesky, and the bundle ID (`app.<name>.mail`).
4. Search engines and social media for negative or slang meanings in major languages.
5. Ask 3–5 target users to say it and spell it after hearing it once.

Decide in Phase 0 ([PLAN.md §9](PLAN.md#9-phases)). The bundle ID can't change after the first store release.
