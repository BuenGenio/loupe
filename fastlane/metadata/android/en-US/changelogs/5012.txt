Loupe's first release on F-Droid (0.2.1 is 0.2.0 built so that F-Droid can check it is identical to ours). Loupe comes in two release types: Free, with no Google code at all, and NonFree, which adds Push through Google's push service. New since the first Nightlies: the app in 37 languages, App Lock, Sign in with Google and Microsoft, JMAP accounts, calendar invitations, S/MIME, and saving messages and folders as files.
