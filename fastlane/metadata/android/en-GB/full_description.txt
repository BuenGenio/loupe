Loupe is a calm, fast mail app with desktop-class tools underneath: expression search, server-side rules, OpenPGP and S/MIME. It talks to your mail server and nobody else.

Simple on the surface. Powerful underneath.
• Readable mode: HTML mail rebuilt to fit your screen: real text sizes, footers as fine print, photos in a swipeable carousel.
• Expression search: Chips or typed queries like from:alice and is:unread. Results from the phone appear at once, the server streams in after.
• Smart Mailboxes that travel: Saved searches stored on your own mail server, so they follow you to every device.
• Rules, on the device or the server: Turn any search into a rule. Run it on the phone, or as Sieve on your server so it works while you sleep.
• Subscriptions: Every newsletter by sender, with read rates and a one-tap unsubscribe centre that runs on your phone.
• Snooze that syncs: Snoozed mail lives in a folder on your server, so other clients that follow the convention see it too.
• Undo and schedule send: Take back a message in the first seconds, or write now and send tomorrow at nine.
• OpenPGP and S/MIME: Encrypt and sign with either standard. OpenPGP works with Thunderbird and Autocrypt.
• Explainable phishing check: Suspicious links and look-alike domains are flagged, with the reason in plain words.
• Calendar invitations: Accept, Maybe or Decline right above the message, with both time zones when they differ.
• IMAP and JMAP: Any IMAP provider with autoconfig, plus native JMAP for Fastmail and Stalwart.
• Tablets and keyboards: Three panes, keyboard shortcuts, drag and drop, and a command palette on Ctrl+K.

There's no Loupe cloud. That's the point.
Your phone talks to your mail server, directly. Loupe has no backend to breach, no analytics to opt out of, and nothing to sell.

Open source, down to the last test.
Every line is public under the MPL-2.0. Nightly builds come straight from GitHub Actions, so what you install is what you can read.

The Loupe app is in English for now; more languages will follow.
